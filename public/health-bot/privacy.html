Last updated: 2026-08-04 · Operated by Zavdi (sole proprietor) · zazesty@gmail.com

Overview

Health Bot is a personal, single-user health companion. It helps the enrolled member review their own clinical information after they authorize access (including via patient-access APIs such as Kaiser Permanente’s Patient Access FHIR APIs), optionally log symptoms or medications, and receive private alerts they configure.

Data we process

  • Member-authorized clinical data (e.g. Patient, Observation, DiagnosticReport and related FHIR resources the member grants).
  • Optional notes the member enters (symptoms, medications, free text).
  • Technical logs needed to run sync and alerts (timestamps, error codes — not sold).

How we use data

Only for the member’s own private review, trend/pattern notes, and personal notifications they enable. No secondary commercial use.

Sharing

We do not sell personal or health data. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing or advertising. Limited processors may see data only as needed to run infrastructure the member chooses (for example cloud LLM providers for narrative review, or a push service for alerts), under the operator’s control and configuration.

Storage & security

Data is stored under the operator’s private control (self-hosted state with restricted filesystem permissions). Tokens and secrets are kept outside public git repositories.

Your choices

You can revoke API authorization at the source (e.g. Kaiser member portal / connected apps), ask the operator to delete local Health Bot state, and disable personal alerts.
