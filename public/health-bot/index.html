Health Bot

Health Bot is a personal health companion operated by Zavdi (sole proprietor). Contact: zazesty@gmail.com.

What it is: a single-user tool for the enrolled member to review their own clinical data after they authorize access (for example via Kaiser Permanente Patient Access FHIR APIs), join optional personal symptom/medication notes, and receive private alerts they configure. It is not a clinic, not multi-patient, and not for marketing.

Who it serves: only the member who authorizes the app with their own credentials / OAuth consent.

Data use: member-authorized records (e.g. labs and related FHIR resources) are used solely for that member’s private review, pattern notes, and optional personal notifications.

We do not sell health data, share it for third-party advertising, or provide care to other people.

Support: zazesty@gmail.com.
