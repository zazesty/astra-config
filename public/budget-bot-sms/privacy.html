1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the Budget Bot text-messaging program (the “SMS Service”). Budget Bot is a personal budget coaching tool that can send spending alerts (for example hardcap warnings, hardcap breaches, and pace warnings).

2. Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number you use to opt in to the SMS Service
  • Opt-in / opt-out status and timestamps related to consent
  • Message metadata needed to send and deliver SMS (for example delivery status from our messaging provider)
  • Support requests you send via HELP or email
  • Where you also use Budget Bot’s budget features, related account and spend-alert preferences used only to generate the alerts you requested (not sold for advertising)

3. How we use information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Send the Budget Bot SMS alerts and program messages you consented to receive
  • Honor STOP / HELP requests and maintain consent records
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the SMS Service
  • Comply with law and carrier / messaging-provider requirements

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You will receive SMS alerts when Budget Bot detects relevant budget events (such as approaching or exceeding your monthly hardcap, or pace warnings). In normal use you should expect fewer than 10 messages per month, though frequency depends on your spending activity and alert settings. You may also receive occasional service or consent-related messages (for example opt-in confirmation or HELP replies).

5. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your wireless carrier may charge standard messaging or data fees for SMS you send or receive. Check your plan with your carrier for details. Budget Bot does not control carrier billing.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

We may disclose information only:

  • To service providers that help us deliver SMS (for example our messaging platform / Twilio) under agreements that limit use to providing those services
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • To protect rights, safety, and security, or to enforce our Terms
  • With your direction or consent

7. Opt-out and HELP

  • Text STOP to the Budget Bot long code at any time to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further program messages unless you opt in again.
  • Text HELP for help, or email zazesty@gmail.com.

8. Data retention

We retain your mobile number and consent records for as long as you remain opted in and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for compliance, dispute resolution, and program integrity. You may request deletion of personal data we control by emailing us, subject to legal retention requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a small personal service (access-controlled systems, restricted credentials, and limited access to messaging configuration). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Children

The SMS Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect mobile numbers from children under 13.
