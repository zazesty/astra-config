Budget Bot SMS

Budget Bot is a personal budget coaching program operated by Zavdi (sole proprietor). Contact: zazesty@gmail.com.

What we text: account-notification SMS only — hardcap near-limit warnings, hardcap breaches, and spending pace warnings for the enrolled subscriber. We do not send marketing or promotional blasts.

Who receives messages: only the mobile number that completed double opt-in (reply YES after the welcome).

Opt-in: text BUDGET or START to the Budget Bot long code, or reply YES to a welcome invitation that includes Privacy, Terms, frequency, and rate disclosures.

Opt-out: text STOP anytime.

Help: text HELP or email zazesty@gmail.com.

Message frequency varies (typically fewer than 10/month). Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share mobile numbers with third parties for their marketing. See Privacy for details.
